Privacy Policy

medai.asos — Dental Clinic Assistant (Instagram)

Who We Are

"medai.asos" is an automated assistant used by a dental clinic to communicate with patients over Instagram Direct Messages. The service is operated by Risqiyev Asadbek Azam o'g'li, an individual entrepreneur registered in Tashkent region, Uzbekistan.

What Information We Collect

  • Your Instagram user ID (as provided by Meta when you message our account).
  • The content of messages you send to the clinic's Instagram account.
  • If you book an appointment: your name and the requested appointment time.

We do not ask for or knowingly collect sensitive medical records, insurance details, or payment information through Instagram messaging.

Why We Collect It

This information is used to:

  • Automatically answer common questions about the clinic (hours, services, location, pricing, etc.).
  • Book, confirm, and manage patient appointments with the clinic.

How Your Information Is Used and Stored

  • Data is stored securely in our database, accessible only to authorized clinic staff and system administrators.
  • Instagram access tokens are encrypted at rest.
  • We do not log or retain the full content of patient messages beyond what is needed to operate the assistant and support the conversation.

Third Parties We Share Data With

Meta / Instagram — used to send and receive messages through the Instagram Messaging API. Meta's own privacy policy governs its handling of your data as the messaging platform.

Google Gemini (AI provider) — the text of your messages is sent to Google's Gemini AI service to generate an automatic reply. Google processes this text solely to produce the response returned to you.

We do not sell your data to any third party, and we do not use your data for advertising.

Medical Disclaimer

This assistant does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or prescriptions. It is intended only for general clinic information and appointment scheduling. Any medical question is redirected to a licensed doctor at the clinic for a proper in-person or professional consultation.

Data Retention and Deletion

We retain conversation and appointment data only as long as needed to provide the service and meet the clinic's recordkeeping needs. If you would like your data deleted, contact us using the details below and we will remove your personal information from our systems, except where retention is required by law.
